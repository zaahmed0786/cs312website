The Garden

The Garden will focus on various flowers and their roles in both the past and the present. This will at minimum include floriography, common symbology, traditional usages, modern utilities, and personal favorites/thoughts. There are a medley of topics I have in the back of my mind that can be further delved into should the need for more material arise. As cliche as it is, I have always had a penchant for flowers. I find them beautiful, powerful, and so much more. In addition, their history (particularly in the Victorian Era) is fascinating in so many different aspects. Even their contemporary usage is varied based on so many aspects including but not limited to culture, region, faith, season, and the types of events. Moreover, the macabre side of flora through poisons and other decaying behavior is a curious facet of nature. I will be developing this floral site throughout the rest of the current summer semester as part of CS 312.
